Your lives

loading

Resume carries a life on from its last checkpoint (taken every 6 months and when stopped), after a crash, a driver reset or a restart. Stop finishes the current step, saves, and ends. Kill ends the process at once; the last checkpoint stays, the current month is lost. Train one life at a time on an 8 GB GPU. While a life trains the laptop is kept from sleeping, but closing the lid or restarting still ends it.

New life

The seed decides everything he is born into: his family and when they die, his friends, his school and his neighbourhood. Type any words as a seed, or press ⚄, then move anything below. What you move stays; the rest follows the seed.

Each factor is seeded from the seed and the family he is born into unless you tick set and choose a value. A schedule changes a factor at a given age. You can change any factor live once he is born.

Talk to him

Say something. He answers with his own network, from the moment he is in and whatever your words bring back to him.
He can only speak as well as he has learned to: a toddler babbles, an adult trained for years makes more sense. Talking to him does not change his life. A life that is not running is woken from its last save, which can take a minute.
